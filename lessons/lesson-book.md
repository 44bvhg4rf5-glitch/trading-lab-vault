# Lesson book

## execution

- With only one execution out of seven theses, the gate-blocking mechanism may be too stringent for choppy regimes, potentially missing valid opportunities. *(ai-post-mortem)*

## macro

- Consumer financial resilience, as measured by the 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances, does not yet translate into a clear shift in equity risk premia. *(continuing-education)*

## markets

- Macro context (rates, debt cycles, liquidity) belongs in every thesis — read the environment, not just the chart. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07)*
- A long-only book in a confirmed downtrend produces repeated failed breakouts, not bargains — trading nothing is the correct output of that regime, not a missed opportunity. *(week-ahead research, 2026-08)*
- When the market's next expected policy move is a hike rather than a cut, soft inflation prints become the bullish catalyst — check which way the surprise has to break before assuming good data is good news. *(week-ahead research, 2026-08)*

## news

- Regulatory enforcement actions can temporarily spike volatility in the targeted sector and its peers. *(continuing-education)*

## process

- Build conviction from research before entering, never after — a thesis is earned by work, then executed with discipline. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07)*
- Frameworks beat signals: never act on someone else's call — understand the setup yourself or skip it. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07)*
- Lifestyle marketing is not a track record — treat unverified profit claims from any influencer source as content, not evidence. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07)*
- Remaining in cash during choppy market regimes preserves capital when underlying strategy baseline signals suffer high failure rates. *(ai-post-mortem)*
- Infrastructure failures (CFD data errors) are blocking valid trades and must be prioritized for resolution. *(ai-post-mortem)*
- Multiple position adoption errors from unrecognizable structures reveal weaknesses in data validation that could lead to unmonitored risks if not addressed. *(ai-post-mortem)*
- High‑frequency news sentiment models that exclude hindsight bias may improve the signal‑to‑noise ratio of momentum strategies. *(continuing-education)*

## psychology

- Markets move in psychological cycles — retail crowds chase what already ran; the edge is anticipating the turn, not joining the stampede. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07)*

## risk

- Concentrated conviction produced his headline wins AND his wipeout at 18 — the same structure does both. This system caps risk per trade instead; the rail is the lesson. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07 (contrast))*
- Holding through a binary event is a bet on luck dressed as conviction — the earnings and macro guards exist to skip exactly that trade. *(study: Julian Petroulas, 2026-07 (contrast))*
- A Fed Chair's set-piece policy speech moves the tape like a decision day even though no rate is set — and a new chair's first one has no prior form to read at all. *(week-ahead research, 2026-08)*
- Spread-based risk gates correctly vetoed GBP_JPY, but need to ensure they're not overly restrictive in volatile conditions. *(ai-post-mortem)*
- The substantial shadow loss of -442 R emphasizes that simulated trading can accumulate risks even without real capital at stake, requiring closer monitoring of shadow metrics. *(ai-post-mortem)*

## timing

- A max-hold window that extends past a known binary event is an unplanned bet on that event — check the calendar against the whole hold period, not just against the entry date. *(week-ahead research, 2026-08)*
- Shadow outcomes show systematic underperformance in momentum strategies despite trending market regime. *(ai-post-mortem)*

## trend

- Strict trend and regime filters effectively block low-probability breakouts when market structure lacks clear direction. *(ai-post-mortem)*
