How to play

  • Zone land as Residential (green), Commercial (blue) or Industrial (yellow). Buildings appear on their own when a zone has power and a road within 3 tiles.
  • Power flows from plants through wires — and through buildings themselves. Roads don't carry power, but you can string a wire across a road (tap the road with the Wire tool). A flashing ⚡ means a tile is unpowered.
  • Watch the R·C·I bars: they show what your citizens want next.
  • Industry pollutes. Keep it downwind of homes; parks and trees clean the air and raise land value.
  • Services (police, fire, school, hospital) raise land value nearby — that's how homes grow into towers.
  • Set taxes in City Hall → Budget. Short on cash? Take a $10,000 bond there ($150/month for 84 months). Roads over water become bridges.
  • Fires can break out — industry and pollution raise the risk, fire stations cut it and put fires out. Burnt lots leave rubble: bulldoze it to rebuild. Struggling buildings go abandoned (boarded up) and recover when conditions improve. Toggle random disasters in City Hall.
  • The 🗺 button cycles data views: power grid, pollution, crime, land value.
  • Controls: tap a tool, then tap or drag on the map to build. Drag with one finger to pan in Look mode, pinch or two-finger drag any time. In Look mode, tap any tile to inspect it.
  • Everything is unlocked from the start. Your city auto-saves on this device.
