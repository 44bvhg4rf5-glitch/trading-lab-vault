## Caveats (read before believing)

- The live mean-reversion strategy also requires NO fresh headlines — untestable historically, so live is stricter than this sim.
- IEX feed history; fills assumed at signal close minus friction. Real slippage varies.
- Momentum has only ~90 days of intraday history on the free feed — treat its numbers as weak evidence.
- No parameter changes are made from this report. It is evidence, not instruction; changes go through the champion/challenger process.
