# Post-mortem 2026-09-02

Today was a masterclass in the value of systematic restraint. While our live portfolio remained virtually flat (+0.07%) with zero executed trades, our shadow system resolved a staggering 525 counterfactual trades, bleeding out a devastating -91.3 R. The market-uptrend filter successfully blocked a barrage of momentum breakouts that would have otherwise decimated our capital in this choppy regime. This is a pure process victory; the hard filters performed exactly as designed to protect us. On the technical front, we experienced persistent API rate-limiting (429 errors) throughout the day. While this did not impact our trading today because the system was sidelined, it represents a critical infrastructure vulnerability that must be resolved before the regime shifts and we need to execute active orders.

## Lessons filed

- **process**: Systematic filters that keep the portfolio sidelined during choppy regimes are just as valuable as those that find winning entries.
- **execution**: API rate limits and infrastructure bottlenecks pose a silent threat to systematic execution and must be proactively managed.
