# Post-mortem 2026-08-31

Today was quiet on paper (+0.60%, 1 sell fill) but a roaring storm in the shadow ledger, which posted a massive +55.7R. Our strict risk gates (blocking the first 15 minutes of the open) and trend filters sidelined a massive wave of winning momentum breakouts in names like WMT, TSLA, and PFE. While it is painful to watch a +55.7R day pass by in the shadows, adhering to the system's hard gates in a regime classified as choppy is correct process, not a failure. However, we faced severe operational friction: repeated AI rate-limiting (429 errors) completely paralyzed thesis generation throughout the session. This infrastructure bottleneck must be resolved to ensure the system can actually generate ideas when the regime shifts.

## Lessons filed

- **process**: Sidelined shadow gains are the necessary cost of maintaining strict risk filters during choppy regimes.
- **execution**: API rate limits and provider quotas represent critical operational risks that can paralyze systematic thesis generation.
