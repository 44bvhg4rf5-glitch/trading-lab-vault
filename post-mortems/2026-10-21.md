# Post-mortem 2026-10-21

Mixed day. Equity rose 0.56% in a trending regime, but only 2 of 9 theses executed. CFD data feed issues (504/401 errors) caused 12 gate blocks - one valid veto (GBP_JPY rejected due to 21.8% spread vs 10% cap). Shadow outcomes showed -431R across 533 trades, indicating the momentum breakout logic is heavily unprofitable in simulation. The system generated signals but infrastructure failures and risk controls prevented execution. Need to investigate why so many shadow trades are losing despite trending market conditions.

## Lessons filed

- **process**: Infrastructure failures (CFD data errors) are blocking valid trades and must be prioritized for resolution.
- **risk**: Spread-based risk gates correctly vetoed GBP_JPY, but need to ensure they're not overly restrictive in volatile conditions.
- **timing**: Shadow outcomes show systematic underperformance in momentum strategies despite trending market regime.

## Hypotheses to test

- The momentum breakout strategy has negative edge in shadow testing due to poor entry timing relative to CFD price feeds
- Data feed reliability issues are causing systematic slippage that erodes the strategy's edge across multiple instruments
